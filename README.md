DHR AI Team - dhimalayanriders-glitch
Central AI lab for D Himalayan Riders & Endeavour Ladakh. We build AI that makes Himalayan motorcycle adventures safer, smoother, and more epic.
Mission
Automate the boring stuff so we can focus on the mountains and the riders.
What This Repo Does
•  Rider AI Assistant - Instant answers on Ladakh routes, permits, altitude, bike prep
•  Glitch Detector - Monitors systems for dhimalayanriders-glitch logs
•  Itinerary Engine - Generates day-wise Ladakh / Spiti / Zanskar plans
•  Content Bot - Auto-generates captions and blogs for @dhimalayanriders
